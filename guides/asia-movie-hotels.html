6 Asian movie hotels you can really book

From a Tokyo skyscraper to a palace on an Indian lake, Asia's grand hotels have had starring roles on screen. These six are open to guests and have a documented place in well-known films.

1. Park Hyatt Tokyo

🗼 Tokyo, Japan · 🎬 Lost in Translation

Sofia Coppola shot Lost in Translation on location here, with the bar scenes set in the New York Bar on the 52nd floor.

📷 moore (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)

2. Taj Lake Palace

🛶 Udaipur, India · 🎬 Octopussy

Octopussy (1983) used Udaipur for its title character's home, and the Lake Palace is widely identified as her lair.

3. Marina Bay Sands

🦁 Singapore, Singapore · 🎬 Crazy Rich Asians

The rooftop SkyPark and infinity pool provide a glamorous backdrop in Crazy Rich Asians (2018).

🦁 Singapore, Singapore · 🎬 Crazy Rich Asians

5. lebua at State Tower

🌆 Bangkok, Thailand · 🎬 The Hangover Part II

The Sky Bar on the 63rd floor served as a filming location for The Hangover Part II.

6. Mount Lavinia Hotel

🌴 Colombo, Sri Lanka · 🎬 The Bridge on the River Kwai

A number of scenes from The Bridge on the River Kwai (1957) were filmed here, and the building stands in for the film's military hospital.

Several of these are city hotels with easy transport, while the Lake Palace and Mount Lavinia are best treated as destinations in themselves. Check the season before you book, as weather varies a lot across the region.
