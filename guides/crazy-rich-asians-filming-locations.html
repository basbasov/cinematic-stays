Crazy Rich Asians hotels you can actually book

Crazy Rich Asians (2018) turned Singapore into a glittering backdrop, and two of its most memorable settings are real hotels. Here is where the film's big moments took place and how to stay at each.

1. Marina Bay Sands

🦁 Singapore, Singapore · 🎬 Crazy Rich Asians

The 57th-floor SkyPark and its huge rooftop infinity pool set the scene for the film's glamorous engagement party, with the city skyline behind it.
