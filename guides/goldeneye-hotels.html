GoldenEye hotels: where it was filmed

Pierce Brosnan's first Bond film (1995) was shot in Monaco, Russia, Puerto Rico and the UK. Two of its hotels are real and open to guests today.

1. Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo

🎰 Monte Carlo, Monaco · 🎬 GoldenEye, Never Say Never Again

GoldenEye's Monaco scenes were filmed at the Monte-Carlo Casino, Port Hercules and the Hôtel de Paris, according to the Monaco Tribune.

2. The Langham, London

🇬🇧 London, United Kingdom · 🎬 GoldenEye, Burnt

The Langham's exterior stood in for the Grand Hotel Europe in St Petersburg, according to Time Out. Interior scenes were not filmed here.
