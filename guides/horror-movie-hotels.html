Real hotels from horror movies you can stay in

The scariest hotel in cinema has real-world counterparts, and both welcome guests. One gave The Shining its look, the other gave Stephen King the idea.

1. Timberline Lodge

🏔️ Oregon, USA · 🎬 The Shining

Stanley Kubrick used the lodge's exterior for the snowbound Overlook in The Shining (1980). The interiors were built on sound stages in England, and there is no Room 237 here.

2. The Stanley Hotel

❄️ Estes Park, USA · 🎬 The Shining (1997 miniseries), Dumb and Dumber

This Colorado hotel inspired Stephen King's original novel, and the 1997 TV miniseries was filmed here. It also played the Danbury Hotel in the comedy Dumb and Dumber.

📷 Renoman58 (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)

Both hotels are far friendlier than their screen reputations suggest, so expect cosy rooms and great mountain views rather than ghosts.
