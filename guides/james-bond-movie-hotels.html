3 James Bond movie hotels you can really book

Bond has checked into plenty of fictional suites, but some of his hotel scenes were filmed in real places that welcome guests today. These three span six decades of the series.

1. Fontainebleau Miami Beach

🌊 Miami, USA · 🎬 Goldfinger, Scarface

Principal photography for Goldfinger (1964) began here, and the hotel appears in the aerial shot after the opening credits.

📷 David Berkowitz (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0)

2. Palácio Estoril Hotel, Golf & Wellness

🌊 Estoril (Cascais), Portugal · 🎬 On Her Majesty's Secret Service

The coastal and hotel scenes before the credits of On Her Majesty's Secret Service were filmed at this Estoril hotel in 1969.

3. Gran Hotel Ciudad de México

🌮 Mexico City, Mexico · 🎬 Spectre

Spectre's Day of the Dead opening was filmed around Mexico City's historic centre, including at this hotel.

📷 Thelmadatter (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)

Each hotel is in a very different setting, from a Miami beachfront to the Portuguese Riviera and Mexico's historic centre, so they work as separate trips rather than one itinerary.
