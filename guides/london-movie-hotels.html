3 London movie hotels you can really book

London's grand hotels have turned up in romances, thrillers and superhero films. These three have a documented place on screen and welcome guests today.

1. The Savoy

🎡 London, United Kingdom · 🎬 Notting Hill

The finale of Notting Hill (1999) takes place at a press conference in the hotel's Lancaster Room.
