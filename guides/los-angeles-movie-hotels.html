5 Los Angeles movie hotels you can really book

Los Angeles has spent a century filming in its own hotels. These five have a documented place in well-known films and are open to guests today.

1. Beverly Wilshire, A Four Seasons Hotel

🌴 Los Angeles, USA · 🎬 Pretty Woman

Pretty Woman (1990) is set in this hotel, although most of its interior scenes were shot at the nearby Ambassador Hotel.

📷 Minnaert (Wikimedia Commons, Public Domain)

2. The Hollywood Roosevelt

🎬 Los Angeles, USA · 🎬 The Fabulous Baker Boys

A scene in The Fabulous Baker Boys (1989) was shot in the Cinegrill nightclub, and the pool appeared in an I Love Lucy episode.

📷 Jason Chang (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)

3. Chateau Marmont

🌃 West Hollywood, USA · 🎬 Somewhere, La La Land

Somewhere (2010) was shot at the hotel, and the building also features in La La Land (2016).

📷 Tony Mariotti (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0)

4. Sunset Tower Hotel

🌴 West Hollywood, United States · 🎬 The Player, Get Shorty

A pivotal scene in The Player (1992) was filmed by the pool, and the tower also appears in Get Shorty (1995).

📷 MikeJiroch (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)

5. The Biltmore Los Angeles (Millennium Biltmore Hotel)

🌴 Los Angeles, USA · 🎬 Ghostbusters

Its ornate rooms stand in for the Sedgwick Hotel in Ghostbusters (1984).

📷 Downtowngal (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)

The hotels sit in different parts of the city, from Downtown to Beverly Hills and the Sunset Strip, so choose the area that suits your trip and visit the others for a drink or a look at the lobby.
