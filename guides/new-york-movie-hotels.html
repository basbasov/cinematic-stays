4 New York movie hotels you can really book

New York has been the backdrop for countless screen stories, and its grand hotels have often played themselves. These four have a documented place in well-known films and shows and are open to guests today.

1. The Plaza, A Fairmont Hotel

🗽 New York, USA · 🎬 Home Alone 2, Sleepless in Seattle

Kevin McCallister checks into The Plaza in Home Alone 2: Lost in New York (1992), and the hotel also appears in North by Northwest.

🗽 New York, United States · 🎬 Week-End at the Waldorf, Scent of a Woman

🗽 New York, United States · 🎬 Scent of a Woman, For Love or Money

The Pierre's Cotillion Ballroom is the location of the tango scene in Scent of a Woman.

4. Lotte New York Palace

🗽 New York, United States · 🎬 Gossip Girl

In Gossip Girl the hotel is depicted as the residence of Serena van der Woodsen.

The Plaza and The Pierre face Central Park, while the Waldorf and the Lotte New York Palace sit in Midtown, so choose the area that suits your trip and visit the others for a drink or a look at the lobby.
