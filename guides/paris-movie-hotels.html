4 Paris movie hotels you can really book

Paris has been a film set for more than a century, and its grand hotels have had plenty of close-ups. These four have a documented place in well-known films and shows and are open to guests today.

1. Hôtel Ritz Paris

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Love in the Afternoon

Love in the Afternoon (1957) is largely set here, and a romantic scene in How to Steal a Million (1966) takes place in the hotel bar.

📷 Vlastimil Juricek (Wikimedia Commons, Public Domain)

2. Hôtel Plaza Athénée

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Sex and the City, Something's Gotta Give, Emily in Paris

The hotel appears in the final season of Sex and the City, as well as Something's Gotta Give and Emily in Paris.

📷 Joe deSousa (Wikimedia Commons, CC0)

3. Hôtel Raphael

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Hotel Chevalier, Place Vendôme

Wes Anderson's short Hotel Chevalier (2007) is set in a hotel room here, and the hotel also appears in Place Vendôme (1998).

📷 Peter Potrowl (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0)

4. Hôtel Regina Louvre

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 The Bourne Identity, La Femme Nikita

The lobby features in The Bourne Identity (2002), when Marie carries out a plan she has agreed with Bourne.

📷 ndiggity from USA (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0)

The Ritz and Plaza Athénée sit in the city's luxury shopping districts, while the Regina is steps from the Louvre and the Raphael close to the Arc de Triomphe. Pick the neighbourhood that suits your itinerary.
