6 romantic movie hotels you can really book

Some of cinema's best love stories happened inside real hotels. These six are still open, still beautiful, and you can book a room for your own scene.

1. Hôtel Ritz Paris

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Love in the Afternoon

Billy Wilder's Love in the Afternoon (1957) largely unfolds in a suite here, where Audrey Hepburn's character falls for Gary Cooper's wealthy American.

📷 Vlastimil Juricek (Wikimedia Commons, Public Domain)

2. Carlton Cannes, a Regent Hotel

🎞️ Cannes, France · 🎬 To Catch a Thief, French Kiss

Hitchcock used the Carlton and its Croisette setting for the 1955 Riviera romance To Catch a Thief, and the 1995 romcom French Kiss was filmed in and around the hotel.

📷 Christophe.Finot (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.5)

Read the full story → Check availability →

3. Hôtel Plaza Athénée

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Sex and the City, Something's Gotta Give, Emily in Paris

Carrie Bradshaw stays here in the Paris finale of Sex and the City, with an Eiffel Tower view from the balcony. The hotel also appears in Emily in Paris.

📷 Joe deSousa (Wikimedia Commons, CC0)

4. Beverly Wilshire, A Four Seasons Hotel

🌴 Los Angeles, USA · 🎬 Pretty Woman

This is the hotel where Vivian and Edward's story unfolds in Pretty Woman, and it still offers a themed suite experience.

📷 Minnaert (Wikimedia Commons, Public Domain)

5. Rosewood Schloss Fuschl

🏰 Fuschl am See, Salzburg, Austria · 🎬 Sissi

The lakeside castle was the setting for the first Sissi film in 1955. Romy Schneider stayed here during filming, and a suite is dedicated to her today.

📷 waterborough (Wikimedia Commons, Public Domain)

6. Marina Bay Sands

🦁 Singapore, Singapore · 🎬 Crazy Rich Asians

The rooftop pool and skyline from Crazy Rich Asians make this a modern favourite for a romantic stay.

📷 Medtd1 (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Whichever you choose, book early for weekends and holidays, and ask about suites with a view.
