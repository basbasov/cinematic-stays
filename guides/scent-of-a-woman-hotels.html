Scent of a Woman hotels: where it was filmed

Al Pacino's Oscar-winning 1992 film sends its characters through some of Manhattan's grandest hotels. Two of them are open today and can be booked.

1. Waldorf Astoria New York

🗽 New York, United States · 🎬 Week-End at the Waldorf, Scent of a Woman

The Waldorf-Astoria is where Frank Slade and Charlie stay during their New York weekend.
