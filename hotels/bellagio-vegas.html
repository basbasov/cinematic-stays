Bellagio, Las Vegas: the Ocean's Eleven hotel

🎰 Las Vegas, USA · 🎬 Ocean's Eleven · 2000s

Few hotels are as closely tied to one film as Bellagio is to Ocean's Eleven. The Las Vegas resort is the main target of Danny Ocean's heist, and you can still visit much of what the cast saw.

On screen: Ocean's Eleven

The 2001 film was shot mostly in Las Vegas between February and June 2001. Producer Jerry Weintraub was friends with the Bellagio's owner at the time, Kirk Kerkorian, which helped the crew get access, and the cast stayed at the hotel during filming. In the story, Bellagio is one of three casinos robbed, along with the Mirage and the MGM Grand.

Fun facts

  • Bellagio opened on 15 October 1998 and cost 1.6 billion dollars, making it the most expensive resort built up to that point.
  • The free fountain show takes place on an 8.5-acre lake, with water jets rising as high as 460 feet.
  • The staircase that Julia Roberts' character descends was later removed to make room for the Spa Tower.
  • A glass ceiling sculpture by Dale Chihuly in the lobby is considered the world's largest glass sculpture.

Visiting today

The fountains and the Conservatory and botanical gardens are free to enjoy. Evening is the best time to see the fountains lit up in front of the hotel.
