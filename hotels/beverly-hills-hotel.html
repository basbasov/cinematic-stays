The Beverly Hills Hotel: the Pink Palace from Troop Beverly Hills

🌴 Beverly Hills, United States · 🎬 Troop Beverly Hills · 1980s

Palm-lined and salmon-pink, the Beverly Hills Hotel has been a Hollywood hangout since 1912. It is also where Shelley Long's troop camps in Troop Beverly Hills.

On screen: Troop Beverly Hills

In Troop Beverly Hills (1989), Shelley Long's character installs her Wilderness Girls troop in a bungalow at the hotel, and the girls roast marshmallows there. Marilyn Monroe and Yves Montand also stayed in two of the bungalows in the late 1950s while filming Let's Make Love.

Fun facts

  • The hotel was built in 1912 by Margaret Anderson, a former manager of the Hollywood Hotel, with architect Elmer Grey.
  • It earned the nickname the Pink Palace after being painted a salmon hue in 1948.
  • The first five private bungalows were built in 1915, and there are around 23 today.
  • The Polo Lounge got its name in 1942, when new owners renamed the El Jardin restaurant to honour polo players.

Visiting today

The bungalows are the closest match to the film, and book up early. The Polo Lounge is a classic place for a drink even if you are not staying.
