Beverly Wilshire, Beverly Hills: the Pretty Woman hotel

🌴 Los Angeles, USA · 🎬 Pretty Woman · 1990s

At the corner of Wilshire Boulevard and Rodeo Drive, the Beverly Wilshire has been a Beverly Hills landmark since 1928. It is best known as the hotel where Julia Roberts and Richard Gere's characters fall for each other in Pretty Woman.

On screen: Pretty Woman

The 1990 romantic comedy is set in this hotel. The lobby scenes, however, were filmed at the Ambassador Hotel in Los Angeles, which has since closed. Production ran from July to October 1989, mostly around Beverly Hills and on Disney studio stages in Burbank.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on 1 January 1928 as the Beverly-Wilshire Apartment Hotel, designed by architects Walker & Eisen in an Italian Renaissance style.
  • Architect Paul Revere Williams led renovations between 1946 and 1957 that added a ballroom and an Olympic-sized pool.
  • It has also appeared in Clueless and in the TV series Entourage.
  • The building has been listed on the U.S. National Register of Historic Places since 1987.

Visiting today

The hotel is run today as Beverly Wilshire, A Four Seasons Hotel, and Rodeo Drive begins right outside the door. Fans should know that the famous lobby on screen was a different building, so go for the setting rather than an exact match.
