Burgh Island Hotel: Agatha Christie's Art Deco island

🏝️ Bigbury-on-Sea, Devon, United Kingdom · 🎬 Evil Under the Sun, Nemesis, Catch Us If You Can · 2000s

Off the Devon coast, an Art Deco hotel sits on a tidal island that you reach on foot at low tide or by sea tractor at high tide. Agatha Christie knew it well.

On screen: Evil Under the Sun

The 2001 Poirot TV adaptation of Evil Under the Sun used the island as a filming location, and several scenes from the BBC's 1987 Miss Marple adaptation Nemesis were filmed in the hotel. Christie's novels Evil Under the Sun and And Then There Were None are both said to have been inspired by the island.

Fun facts

  • The Art Deco hotel was completed in 1929 for the film-maker Archibald Nettlefold, and Wikipedia credits the architect as Matthew Dawson.
  • Nettlefold bought the island in 1927; a small monastery had existed there around the 1330s.
  • The hotel has 25 rooms, one built from the captain's cabin of HMS Ganges (1821).
  • The building is Grade II listed.
  • The hotel's Beach House was reportedly built for Agatha Christie, who had her home at Greenway nearby.

Visiting today

Check the tide timetable before travelling. Evening dress codes and Art Deco dinners are part of the experience.
