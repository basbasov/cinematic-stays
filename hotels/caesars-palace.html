Caesars Palace, Las Vegas: the hotel from The Hangover

🎰 Las Vegas, USA · 🎬 The Hangover · 2000s

Roman statues, marble columns and a driveway made for grand entrances: Caesars Palace has been a Las Vegas icon since 1966. In 2009 it became the home base of the missing groom's wild weekend in The Hangover.

On screen: The Hangover

Most of The Hangover was shot on location at the hotel, including the front desk, lobby, suite, entrance drive, pools, corridors, elevators and roof. The wrecked suite seen on screen was built on a soundstage, and the Nevada part of the shoot lasted fifteen days. The sequel, The Hangover Part III, also featured the hotel, and a suite from that film is now available for guest stays.

Fun facts

  • Caesars Palace opened on 5 August 1966 with a Roman Empire theme throughout.
  • Evel Knievel attempted a jump over its fountains in 1967 and was seriously injured.
  • The resort has about 3,960 rooms and suites spread across six towers.
  • Earlier films shot here include Rocky III and Rain Man.

Visiting today

The hotel is huge, so allow time to walk between the entrance, pools and casino floor. If you want a film-themed stay, ask about the suite from The Hangover Part III.
