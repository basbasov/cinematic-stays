Carlton Cannes: the Croisette hotel from To Catch a Thief

🎞️ Cannes, France · 🎬 To Catch a Thief, French Kiss · 1950s

With its twin domes dominating the Croisette, the Carlton is the face of Cannes. Hitchcock and Hollywood romcoms have both used its Riviera glamour.

On screen: To Catch a Thief

The Carlton is credited with a role in Hitchcock's To Catch a Thief (1955), with Grace Kelly and Cary Grant, and Wikipedia says the 1995 romcom French Kiss was filmed in and around the hotel. Grace Kelly was photographed on its terrace in 1955.

Fun facts

  • The hotel was built for the Swiss hotelier Henry Ruhl in the early 1910s.
  • It has been tied to the Cannes Film Festival since 1946, when it hosted a small group of journalists.
  • It hosted the G20 summit in 2011.
  • The French government has declared it a national monument.
  • It reopened in March 2023 after renovation as a Regent hotel.

Visiting today

Rooms fill up far ahead during the film festival in May. Outside it, the Croisette is quiet and good for sea views.
