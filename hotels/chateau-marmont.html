Chateau Marmont: Hollywood's castle on Sunset Boulevard

🌃 West Hollywood, USA · 🎬 Somewhere, La La Land · 2010s

Modelled on a French château, the Chateau Marmont has been a discreet retreat for people in the film business since 1929. It is a real hotel, and you can book it.

On screen: Somewhere

Sofia Coppola filmed Somewhere here, with the hotel at the centre of the story of a movie star's aimless days. In La La Land (2016), the hotel's exterior is the place where Mia stays, although the film's interior scenes were reportedly shot elsewhere.

Fun facts

  • The Chateau Marmont opened in 1929 as an apartment-hotel hybrid.
  • It was built to replicate the Château d'Amboise, a royal retreat in France's Loire Valley.
  • Hollywood producer Albert E. Smith purchased the hotel in 1929.
  • John Belushi died in a bungalow here in March 1982.
  • Shawn Levy's 2019 book The Castle on Sunset tells the hotel's history in detail.

Visiting today

The hotel is on Sunset Boulevard in West Hollywood, close to restaurants and shops. Bungalows and garden rooms book out far ahead.
