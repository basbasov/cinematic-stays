Çırağan Palace Kempinski: the Ottoman palace linked to Skyfall

🕌 Istanbul, Turkey · 🎬 Skyfall · 2010s

Right on the Bosphorus, the Çırağan Palace was built for an Ottoman sultan and is now a luxury hotel. It welcomed the Skyfall cast to Istanbul in 2012.

On screen: Skyfall

Daniel Craig and the Skyfall team held their press conference and photo call at the Çırağan Palace Kempinski on April 29, 2012, when the Bond film came to Istanbul. Sources disagree on whether any scenes were shot on its grounds, so treat it as a base for the production rather than a confirmed set.

Fun facts

  • The palace was completed in about 1871 under Sultan Abdülaziz.
  • Abdülaziz was found dead inside in 1876, after being dethroned.
  • In 1909 the Ottoman Parliament was allowed to meet at the palace.
  • A fire in January 1910 left only the outer walls standing.
  • A restoration beginning in 1986 turned the palace into a hotel.

Visiting today

Rooms facing the Bosphorus are the highlight. The palace is a short ride from Istanbul's old city sights.
