The Dolder Grand: the Zurich hotel from The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo

🏔️ Zurich, Switzerland · 🎬 The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo · 2010s

The Dolder Grand is a fairy-tale hotel on a hillside above Zurich, opened in 1899 and rebuilt for the modern age by Norman Foster. In 2011 it appeared in David Fincher's thriller The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo.

On screen: The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo

Switzerland Tourism lists the hotel among the places in Zurich where the film was shot, alongside the airport and the old town. In the story, Lisbeth Salander arrives at the hotel after helping Mikael Blomkvist clear his name, ready to meet the bankers face to face and empty the accounts of the financier Hans-Erik Wennerstrom.

Fun facts

  • The hotel was built from 1897 to 1899 to a design by the Basel architect Jacques Gros.
  • It opened on 10 May 1899 as the Dolder Grand Hotel & Curhaus, for the restaurateur Heinrich Hürlimann.
  • It closed in 2004 for a renovation led by Norman Foster and reopened on 3 April 2008.
  • The renovation restored the 1899 appearance and added two new wings and two floors below ground.

Visiting today

The hotel stands on a hill above Zurich, and its spa and art collection make it a place to stay rather than just photograph. Check dates on Booking.com if you want to follow Lisbeth's route to Zurich.
