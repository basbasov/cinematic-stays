The Don CeSar: the pink palace from Once Upon a Time in America and The Infiltrator

🌴 St. Pete Beach, United States · 🎬 Once Upon a Time in America, H.E.A.L.T.H., The Infiltrator · 1980s

Rising above the sand on Florida's Gulf coast, the Don CeSar is hard to miss: a pink Mediterranean-style hotel that locals simply call the Don. Film crews have been drawn to it for decades.

On screen: Once Upon a Time in America

The St Pete Catalyst reports that Sergio Leone filmed a two-day beach scene behind the hotel in October 1982 for Once Upon a Time in America, with Robert De Niro, James Woods and about 350 local extras. Robert Altman's H.E.A.L.T.H. (1980) used the hotel as a centerpiece, according to WUSF. For The Infiltrator, Creative Loafing Tampa describes Bryan Cranston filming at the pool deck, where the hotel stood in for a Miami Beach resort.

Fun facts

  • The Don CeSar opened on January 16, 1928, with a party for the elite of Tampa and St. Petersburg.
  • During World War II the U.S. Army took over the hotel and converted it into a military hospital.
  • After a sale in 1972 it reopened in November 1973 and was added to the National Register of Historic Places on April 3, 1975.
  • Wikipedia also lists Forever Mine (1999) among the productions filmed at the hotel.

Visiting today

The hotel sits right on the beach on St. Pete Beach, so the setting from the Leone scene is easy to recognise. Book ahead for winter and spring, when the Gulf coast is busiest.
