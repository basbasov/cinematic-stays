The Esplanade Zagreb: the real hotel from Canary Black

🏙️ Zagreb, Croatia · 🎬 Canary Black, The Winds of War · 2020s

The Esplanade has welcomed guests in the Croatian capital since 1925, originally as a stop for Orient Express travellers. Film fans may know it from the spy thriller Canary Black.

On screen: Canary Black

Canary Black, the action thriller starring Kate Beckinsale and Rupert Friend, shot exterior and interior scenes at the hotel, and the Filming in Croatia site lists the Esplanade among the film's Zagreb locations. The hotel also appeared earlier in the 1983 miniseries The Winds of War and in the 1988 television film The Great Escape II: The Untold Story.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on 22 April 1925 to house passengers arriving on the Orient Express.
  • Otto Rehnig won the design competition, and Dionis Sunko modified the plans; many now regard Sunko as the architect.
  • It closed in 2002 for a major renovation and reopened in May 2004 as the Regent Esplanade Zagreb.
  • Since 2012 it has operated independently under its current name.
