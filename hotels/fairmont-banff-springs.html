Fairmont Banff Springs: the castle hotel from River of No Return

🏔️ Banff, Canada · 🎬 River of No Return · 1950s

Set among the Canadian Rockies, the Banff Springs looks like a Scottish castle that lost its way. In 1953 it welcomed Marilyn Monroe and Joe DiMaggio while she filmed River of No Return nearby.

On screen: River of No Return

River of No Return (1954) used the Rockies around Banff for its Western scenery. Monroe and DiMaggio stayed at the Banff Springs during the shoot, and one account says she rested here after spraining her ankle while filming in the park.

Fun facts

  • The hotel officially opened on June 1, 1888, built by the Canadian Pacific Railway under general manager William Cornelius Van Horne.
  • It was placed beside hot springs that the railway promoted as therapeutic waters.
  • Its style is Scottish Baronial, which is why it is nicknamed the Castle in the Rockies.
  • It is a National Historic Site of Canada.
  • It carried the Canadian Pacific name until 2000, when it joined the Fairmont chain.

Visiting today

Banff National Park is lovely in every season, from ski winters to summer hiking. Rooms with mountain views fill up first in July and August.
