Fairmont San Francisco: the real hotel behind the TV series Hotel

🌉 San Francisco, United States · 🎬 Hotel · 1980s

Crowning Nob Hill since 1907, the Fairmont is one of San Francisco's grandest addresses. Television viewers of the 1980s may know it as the fictional St. Gregory Hotel from the ABC drama Hotel.

On screen: Hotel

The series Hotel, based on an Arthur Hailey novel, is set in a San Francisco hotel called the St. Gregory. Establishing shots were filmed in front of the Fairmont, and some interiors for the 1983 pilot were shot inside. When the show was picked up, a smaller replica of the lobby was built on a studio lot in Burbank for regular filming.

Fun facts

  • The Fairmont opened on 18 April 1907, one year after the 1906 earthquake and fire had gutted the original building.
  • Julia Morgan was hired to repair the building after the disaster because of her use of reinforced concrete.
  • The hotel's Tower wing opened in 1962, and the Tonga Room is still open today.
  • The ABC series Hotel ran from September 1983 to May 1988.

Visiting today

The Fairmont sits on Mason Street at the top of Nob Hill, and its Tonga Room is still open for visitors. Check current room types and rates on Booking.com before you plan your stay.
