Fontainebleau Miami Beach: the hotel from Goldfinger and Scarface

🌊 Miami, USA · 🎬 Goldfinger, Scarface · 1960s

The curved white Fontainebleau became a symbol of Miami Beach glamour after it opened in 1954. Two very different films put it on screen: a James Bond classic and a gangster epic.

On screen: Goldfinger

Principal photography for Goldfinger began at the hotel in January 1964, and its Miami scenes were shot there. The hotel appears in the sweeping aerial shot after the opening credits and is where Jill Masterson meets her fate in the film. It also features in a well-known scene in Scarface (1983), though the hotel is not named in that film.

Fun facts

  • Architect Morris Lapidus, known for wearing bow ties, worked bow-tie shapes into his design.
  • The Florida chapter of the American Institute of Architects ranked it first on its 2012 list of 100 notable Florida places.
  • The hotel was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2008.
  • Other productions shot here include The Bodyguard and an episode of The Sopranos.

Visiting today

The Fontainebleau is a large beachfront resort, so a stay is as much about the pool and the architecture as the films. Take a walk through the lobby for a look at the Lapidus design.
