Grand Wailea: the Maui resort from Just Go with It

🌺 Maui, USA · 🎬 Just Go with It · 2010s

Stretched along Wailea Beach, the Grand Wailea is one of Hawaii's most lavish resorts. Hollywood noticed in 2010, when the Adam Sandler and Jennifer Aniston comedy Just Go with It came to Maui.

On screen: Just Go with It

Just Go with It was shot on Maui and Kauai in the spring of 2010, and the Honolulu Star-Advertiser reports that part of the film, including a luau scene, was filmed at the Grand Wailea. The resort's tropical grounds and pools make it a natural stand-in for the film's luxury Hawaiian getaway.

Fun facts

  • The resort opened in 1991 as the Grand Hyatt Wailea, built by Japanese developer Takeshi Sekiguchi.
  • It was reported at the time to be among the most expensive hotels ever built.
  • Sekiguchi was an art collector, and the property holds pieces by artists including Fernando Botero as well as local Maui artists.
  • It has been part of Hilton's Waldorf Astoria collection since 2007.

Visiting today

Wailea has calm beaches and reliable weather year-round, with the busiest periods around winter holidays. Families should ask about the resort's pool complex when booking.
