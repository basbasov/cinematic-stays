Grandhotel Pupp: the real Hotel Splendide from Casino Royale

🏰 Karlovy Vary, Czech Republic · 🎬 Casino Royale · 2000s

Karlovy Vary's grand spa hotel has welcomed guests for centuries, and in 2006 it became a James Bond location. Its façade is the Hotel Splendide in Casino Royale.

On screen: Casino Royale

In Casino Royale (2006), the Grandhotel Pupp appears as the Hotel Splendide in Montenegro, the setting where Bond and Vesper share their dinner. The real Hotel Splendide in Montenegro opened years after the film and is a different property.

Fun facts

  • The hotel's roots go back to 1701, when a Saxon Hall, the town's first venue for social gatherings, was built on the site.
  • Anton, Julius and Heinrich Pupp became official business partners in 1872.
  • The hotel regained the name Grandhotel Pupp in 1990, and its owners credit six generations of the Pupp family with shaping it.
  • It is reported to have been an inspiration for Wes Anderson's The Grand Budapest Hotel.

Visiting today

Karlovy Vary hosts a well-known international film festival in early summer, when rooms are scarce. The town is also famous for its thermal spring colonnades.
