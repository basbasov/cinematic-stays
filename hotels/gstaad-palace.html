Gstaad Palace: the Swiss castle hotel from The Pink Panther

🏔️ Gstaad, Switzerland · 🎬 The Return of the Pink Panther, The Palace · 1970s

Above the Swiss resort of Gstaad, the Palace looks like a fairytale castle. It has welcomed film stars and royals since 1913 and appears on screen.

On screen: The Return of the Pink Panther

Blake Edwards filmed parts of The Return of the Pink Panther here in 1975, and the exterior appears in the film with Peter Sellers as Inspector Clouseau, who is famously trapped in the hotel's revolving door. Roman Polanski's The Palace (2023) is set in Gstaad and centred on a hotel of the same name.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on December 8, 1913, after ground was broken in April 1912.
  • The building is reported to have cost 2.5 million Swiss francs.
  • The Scherz family runs the hotel, and have done so for generations.
  • Its castle-like silhouette above Gstaad is easy to spot from the village.

Visiting today

Gstaad is a ski resort in winter and a calm alpine retreat in summer. Book early for the Christmas and New Year weeks.
