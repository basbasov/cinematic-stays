Hilton London Hyde Park: the Coburg Hotel from Hitchcock's Frenzy

🎡 London, United Kingdom · 🎬 Frenzy · 1970s

Overlooking Hyde Park on Bayswater Road, this hotel was known as the Coburg when Alfred Hitchcock came to film there. Its brief but memorable appearance is in his 1972 thriller Frenzy.

On screen: Frenzy

In Frenzy, the fugitive Richard Blaney takes Babs Milligan to the Coburg, where they sign in as Mr. and Mrs. Oscar Wilde. Filming took place at the hotel in September 1971, though most of the interiors were recreated at Pinewood Studios. The shot of the police looking at the fire escape was filmed from a fifth-floor room.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened in 1907 as the Coburg Court Hotel.
  • It was renamed the Coburg Hotel in the early 1960s.
  • It has carried the Hilton name since July 1999.
  • The hotel has 136 rooms and looks out over Hyde Park and Kensington Gardens.
