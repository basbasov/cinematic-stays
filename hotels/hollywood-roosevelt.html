The Hollywood Roosevelt: where the first Oscars were held

🎬 Los Angeles, USA · 🎬 The Fabulous Baker Boys · 1980s

Across from the TCL Chinese Theatre, the Hollywood Roosevelt is a Hollywood Boulevard landmark with an unusually good origin story: it hosted the very first Academy Awards.

On screen: The Fabulous Baker Boys

The hotel has been a screen location for decades. The famous piano scene in The Fabulous Baker Boys (1989) was shot in its Cinegrill nightclub, and its poolside has appeared in productions including I Love Lucy, Almost Famous and Entourage.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened in 1927 and was backed by Hollywood figures including Douglas Fairbanks, Mary Pickford, Sid Grauman and Louis B. Mayer.
  • The first Academy Awards were held in its Blossom Ballroom on May 16, 1929, with about 270 guests and no broadcast.
  • Wings won the first Best Picture award, and a poster for the film still hangs on the hotel's lower level.
  • Marilyn Monroe lived in a second-floor cabana overlooking the pool for two years and shot her first print ad at the hotel.
  • The hotel is named after President Theodore Roosevelt.

Visiting today

Ask about poolside rooms with a view of the Tropicana Pool. Hollywood Boulevard's attractions are within walking distance.
