Hotel Adlon Berlin: the real hotel from Unknown

🐻 Berlin, Germany · 🎬 Unknown · 2010s

Standing beside the Brandenburg Gate, the Adlon is Berlin's best-known grand hotel. Film fans may recognise it as the place where Liam Neeson's character loses his identity in the 2011 thriller Unknown.

On screen: Unknown

In Unknown, Dr. Martin Harris and his wife arrive in Berlin and check in at the Adlon, and much of the story takes place at the hotel. Wikipedia notes that some of the shooting was done there, and the film-location site Movie-Locations ties the Adlon to the arrival scene and to the moment Harris is turned away after finding another man has taken his place. The rest of the film was shot at other Berlin sites and at Studio Babelsberg in early 2010.

Fun facts

  • The original Adlon opened in 1907 and was largely destroyed by fire in 1945, with its last remains demolished in 1984.
  • The present hotel opened on 23 August 1997 and was designed by Rainer Michael Klotz.
  • It stands at the corner of Unter den Linden and Pariser Platz, directly opposite the Brandenburg Gate.
  • The hotel's story also inspired a 1955 film, Hotel Adlon, based on a book by Hedda Adlon.

Visiting today

The location puts you steps from the Brandenburg Gate and Unter den Linden, so most of central Berlin is within walking distance. Rooms facing Pariser Platz are the ones to ask about.
