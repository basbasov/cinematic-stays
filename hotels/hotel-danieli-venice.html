Hotel Danieli: the Venice palazzo from two James Bond films

🛶 Venice, Italy · 🎬 From Russia with Love · 1960s

Facing the lagoon on the Riva degli Schiavoni, Hotel Danieli is a Gothic palazzo that has been a hotel since the 1820s. James Bond has visited it more than once.

On screen: From Russia with Love

The hotel appears in From Russia with Love (1963), where Bond and Tatiana check in after the job is done. It appears again in Moonraker (1979), when Roger Moore's Bond follows a lead on the industrialist Hugo Drax. The lobby bar is a favourite with Bond fans.

Fun facts

  • The central wing, Palazzo Dandolo, dates to the late 14th century.
  • A hotelier first rented part of the palazzo in the 1820s, and it has been a hotel since.
  • Today the complex includes two 19th-century additions linked to the original palazzo by bridges.
  • It now operates as Danieli, Venezia, a Four Seasons Hotel, with around 120 rooms and suites.

Visiting today

Venice is at its quietest outside summer and carnival season. Ask for a lagoon-view room to see the same water the Bond films show.
