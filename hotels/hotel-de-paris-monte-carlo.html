Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo: the Bond hotel on Casino Square

🎰 Monte Carlo, Monaco · 🎬 GoldenEye, Never Say Never Again · 1990s

Standing on the square beside the Monte-Carlo Casino, the Hôtel de Paris is the symbol of Monaco's Belle Époque glamour, and a natural stop for James Bond.

On screen: GoldenEye

GoldenEye (1995) filmed in Monaco at the Monte-Carlo Casino, Port Hercules and the Hôtel de Paris, which also housed the cast during the shoot. The hotel is also reported to appear in the 1983 Bond film Never Say Never Again, though that link is less well documented.

Fun facts

  • The hotel was completed in 1864 on the Spélugues plateau, an idea credited to the businessman François Blanc.
  • The neighbouring Café de Paris joined the square in 1868.
  • The Casino Square is now pedestrianised, so cars no longer drive in front of the hotel.
  • The hotel is known for its wine cellar.
  • The hotel is part of the Société des Bains de Mer group, which also runs the casino and the opera house.

Visiting today

Rooms facing the square give the best view of the casino, and the Casino Square is a short stroll from the harbour. Expect higher prices and tight availability during the Monaco Grand Prix in May.
