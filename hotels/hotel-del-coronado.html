Hotel del Coronado: the Victorian beach resort from Some Like It Hot

🏖️ San Diego, USA · 🎬 Some Like It Hot · 1950s

The red-roofed turrets of the Hotel del Coronado have watched over the Pacific since 1888. Billy Wilder chose them as the setting for one of the best-loved comedies in film history.

On screen: Some Like It Hot

Some Like It Hot was made in California in 1958 and released in 1959. The hotel stood in for the fictional Seminole Ritz, a Florida resort, partly because its look suited the film's 1920s setting and it was close to Hollywood. The hotel had been on screen as early as 1918 and has appeared in many films since.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on 19 February 1888 and was then the largest resort hotel in the world.
  • It was designed by James W. Reid in the Queen Anne style, and is one of the few surviving wooden Victorian beach resorts.
  • The wooden ceiling of the Crown Room was fitted with pegs and glue instead of nails.
  • In 1904 it introduced the world's first electrically lit outdoor Christmas tree.
  • It was named a National Historic Landmark in 1977.

Visiting today

The grounds and beach are part of the draw, so allow time for a walk around the verandas as well as the lobby. Rooms in the historic main building feel closest to the film's era.
