Hotel Gellért: Budapest's Art Nouveau landmark from Red Sparrow

🏛️ Budapest, Hungary · 🎬 Red Sparrow, I Spy, Mephisto · 2010s

At the foot of Gellért Hill, beside the Danube, the Hotel Gellért is the grand Art Nouveau hotel of Budapest. Its Secessionist halls have been popular with film crews.

On screen: Red Sparrow

Listings connect the hotel to the spy thriller Red Sparrow (2018), the comedy I Spy (2002) and the Oscar-winning drama Mephisto (1981). Elizabeth Taylor and Richard Burton famously stayed at the Gellért while filming in Budapest.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on 26 September 1918, as the first luxury hotel in Budapest.
  • It was designed by Ármin Hegedűs, Artúr Sebestyén and Izidor Sterk.
  • Early features included in-room telephones and piped thermal water.
  • The hotel suffered significant damage in the siege of Budapest in 1945.
  • The Gellért Baths next door have been closed since October 2025 for refurbishment, with a planned reopening in 2028.

Visiting today

Check the baths' status before you book, as they are closed for renovation. The hotel is a short tram ride from the city centre.
