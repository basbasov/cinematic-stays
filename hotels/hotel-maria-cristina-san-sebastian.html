Hotel María Cristina, San Sebastián: the hotel from Rifkin's Festival

🏖️ San Sebastián, Spain · 🎬 Rifkin's Festival · 2020s

With its Belle Époque façade on the Urumea riverbank, the Hotel María Cristina is one of the grandest addresses in San Sebastián. Film fans know it as the place where the characters of Woody Allen's Rifkin's Festival stay during the city's film festival.

On screen: Rifkin's Festival

Rifkin's Festival was shot in San Sebastián between July and August 2019, and the hotel plays a major part in the story as the base for the festival guests. The city's tourist board and Spain's national tourism site both point to the hotel as a location, though neither says which scenes were filmed inside it. Allen's family first stayed in a rented house in Igeldo during the shoot and then moved into the María Cristina.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on 9 July 1912; its plans were drawn up by Charles Mewès, the French architect known for Ritz hotels.
  • After a full restoration, the hotel reopened in 1987 with a concert by José Carreras.
  • Rifkin's Festival had its world premiere at the San Sebastián International Film Festival in September 2020.

Visiting today

The hotel stands on Plaza Okendo, facing the Victoria Eugenia Theatre. Book well ahead for late September, when the film festival fills the city.
