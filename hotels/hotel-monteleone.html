Hotel Monteleone: the New Orleans hotel with the spinning bar

🎷 New Orleans, USA · 🎬 Double Jeopardy · 1990s

In the French Quarter, Hotel Monteleone is known for a bar that slowly rotates while you drink. It is also family-run since 1886, and it has appeared on screen.

On screen: Double Jeopardy

In the 1999 thriller Double Jeopardy, scenes were shot in the hotel's lobby and at its front entrance. The Carousel Bar was dressed up as an Armani shop for the scene in which Ashley Judd's character picks up a dress.

Fun facts

  • The Monteleone family has run the hotel for five generations, since Antonio Monteleone bought a small hotel here in 1886.
  • The hotel took its present name in 1908, after 300 rooms were added.
  • The Carousel Bar opened on September 3, 1949, and revolves once every 15 minutes.
  • Fiber optics added to the ceiling in 1992 create the effect of stars above the bar.
  • William Faulkner made a point of staying here, and later writers including Anne Rice and John Grisham have too.

Visiting today

Grab a seat at the Carousel Bar early in the evening, before it fills up. The hotel is a short walk from Jackson Square and Bourbon Street.
