Hotel New Otani Tokyo: the Osato Chemicals hotel from You Only Live Twice

🗼 Tokyo, Japan · 🎬 You Only Live Twice · 1960s

Opened in 1964 for the Tokyo Olympics, the Hotel New Otani is a landmark in Chiyoda City with a 400-year-old garden at its centre. In 1967 it became a Bond villain's headquarters.

On screen: You Only Live Twice

In You Only Live Twice (1967), the hotel appears as the Tokyo headquarters of Osato Chemical & Engineering, the Japanese front for Blofeld's SPECTRE organisation. The building seen in the film is the hotel's Main building, recognisable by the disc on its roof. Bond enters the company twice, first as an infiltrating spy and later disguised as an investor.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on 1 September 1964, just before the Tokyo Olympics.
  • It was built in seventeen months and was the tallest building in Tokyo until 1968.
  • The site was once the residence of the samurai lord Katō Kiyomasa, and the 400-year-old garden was kept as part of the hotel.
  • The hotel has 1,479 rooms and 39 restaurants, spread across the Main building and the later Garden Tower.

Visiting today

The hotel sits in the Kioicho district of Chiyoda City, and the garden is a calm place to walk before or after a Bond tour of the city. Make sure to book the Main building if you want to stay in the tower seen on screen.
