Hôtel Raphael: the Paris hotel from Hotel Chevalier

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Hotel Chevalier, Place Vendôme · 2000s

A short walk from the Arc de Triomphe, the Hôtel Raphael has been welcoming guests on Avenue Kléber since 1925. Film fans know it as the setting of Wes Anderson's Hotel Chevalier.

On screen: Hotel Chevalier

Hotel Chevalier (2007) is a short film set almost entirely in a Paris hotel room, where Jack, who has been hiding out, is surprised when his ex-girlfriend Rhett turns up unannounced. It works as the prologue to The Darjeeling Limited, taking place two weeks before Jack joins his brothers in India, and it ends with the pair on a balcony overlooking Paris.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened in 1925 and is named as a tribute to the painter Raphael.
  • It is an Art Deco building with 83 bedrooms over seven floors, and some rooms face the Eiffel Tower or the Arc de Triomphe.
  • Hotel Chevalier lasts about 13 minutes and stars Jason Schwartzman and Natalie Portman.
  • The hotel also appears in other films, among them Love in Paris (1996) and Place Vendôme (1998).

Visiting today

The hotel sits close to the Champs-Élysées and the Arc de Triomphe, so it works well as a base for a first visit to Paris. Ask for a room with a view if you want to echo the balcony scene.
