Hôtel Regina Louvre: the Paris hotel from The Bourne Identity

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 The Bourne Identity, La Femme Nikita · 2000s

Facing the Tuileries Garden on Place des Pyramides, the Régina has been a Paris landmark since the 1900 World's Fair. It also turns up in several well-known thrillers.

On screen: The Bourne Identity

In The Bourne Identity (2002), the hotel is one of the Paris locations: Marie goes down to the lobby to carry out a plan she has worked out with Bourne. Wikipedia's list of the hotel's screen credits also includes La Femme Nikita (1990), Kiss of the Dragon (2001) and RED 2 (2013).

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened in 1900 to coincide with the Paris World's Fair.
  • The Second Empire building was constructed in 1898-1900 by Léonard Tauber and Constant Baverez.
  • It stands across Rue de Rivoli from the Tuileries Garden, close to an entrance to the Louvre.
  • A gilded statue of Joan of Arc on horseback stands in the square in front of the hotel.

Visiting today

The location is hard to beat for museum days, with the Louvre and the Tuileries both a few steps away. Look for the Joan of Arc statue outside, then step into the lobby to compare it with the film.
