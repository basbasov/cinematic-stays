Hotel Sacher Vienna: the hotel of the original Sachertorte

🎻 Vienna, Austria · 🎬 Das Sacher, Hotel Sacher · 2010s

Behind the Vienna State Opera, the Hotel Sacher is known for a chocolate cake and a very theatrical history. Both have been on screen.

On screen: Das Sacher

The 2016 Austrian-German two-part drama Das Sacher tells the story of Anna Sacher, the widow of the hotel's founder, around 1900. A report says it was filmed in the Hotel Sacher itself. The hotel also gave its name to a 1939 film, Hotel Sacher.

Fun facts

  • Eduard Sacher founded the hotel in 1876, and his wife Anna ran it after he died in 1892.
  • The Sachertorte is credited to Eduard's father Franz Sacher, who is said to have created it for a reception given by Chancellor Metternich.
  • After years of litigation with the confectioner Demel, only the Sacher dessert may be marketed as the Original Sachertorte.
  • The hotel stands in Vienna's first district, behind the State Opera.

Visiting today

Try the Sachertorte in the hotel's café, and book a room early for the opera season. Vienna's Ringstrasse sights are within walking distance.
