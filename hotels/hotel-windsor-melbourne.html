The Hotel Windsor: Phryne Fisher's Melbourne home

🇦🇺 Melbourne, Australia · 🎬 Miss Fisher's Murder Mysteries · 2010s

Across from Parliament House on Spring Street, the Hotel Windsor is one of the few grand Victorian hotels still standing in Australia. It is also where Miss Fisher's Murder Mysteries begins.

On screen: Miss Fisher's Murder Mysteries

In the series, Phryne Fisher checks into the Hotel Windsor on arriving in Melbourne, and is embroiled in mystery almost as soon as she does. The hotel's period interiors suit the show's 1920s setting.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened in 1883, designed by Charles Webb for the shipping magnate George Nipper, and was first called the Grand Hotel.
  • It was renamed Windsor in 1920.
  • Melbourne's Spring Street setting places it opposite Parliament House.
  • It is reported to have hosted delegates drafting Australia's Constitution in 1898.
  • Oberoi Hotels took over the lease in 1980.
  • One source describes it as Australia's only surviving grand 19th-century city hotel.

Visiting today

The hotel is known for its afternoon tea, and Spring Street is within walking distance of the city's theatres and Parliament Gardens. Heritage rooms with period detail are the closest match to the show.
