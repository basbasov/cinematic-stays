Jumeirah Zabeel Saray: the Mumbai palace from Ghost Protocol

🏙️ Dubai, United Arab Emirates · 🎬 Mission: Impossible – Ghost Protocol · 2010s

Modelled on Ottoman palaces and set on Dubai's Palm Jumeirah, the Zabeel Saray looks like a film set, and Mission: Impossible - Ghost Protocol used it as one.

On screen: Mission: Impossible – Ghost Protocol

In Ghost Protocol (2011), the black-tie party hosted in a Mumbai palace by Anil Kapoor's playboy billionaire was filmed at the Zabeel Saray in Dubai. The hotel was still in its pre-opening phase, and the shoot took three days. Its general manager recalled that they had planned to open at the same time as the production wanted to film.

Fun facts

  • The resort sits on the West Crescent of the Palm Jumeirah.
  • Its interiors are modelled on the palaces of the Ottoman Empire, with hand-painted finishes and Turkish artworks.
  • Filming in Dubai took 23 days in total, across locations including Deira, Satwa and the Dubai International Financial Centre.
  • Kapoor described the sequence as taking place in his Indian palace.

Visiting today

Dubai is hottest in summer, so winter is the best time for a beach resort stay. The Palm Jumeirah is a short taxi ride from Dubai Marina.
