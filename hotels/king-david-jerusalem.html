King David Hotel Jerusalem: the real hotel from Exodus

🕍 Jerusalem, Israel · 🎬 Exodus · 1960s

Built from pink Jerusalem limestone, the King David Hotel has watched over the Old City since 1931. It also has a small part in film history: Otto Preminger's Exodus shot scenes here.

On screen: Exodus

Several scenes of the 1960 film Exodus were shot at the hotel, outside as well as inside, including in the main lobby and on the terrace. A scene starring Paul Newman was filmed on that terrace. The film as a whole was shot on location around Israel, including Jerusalem.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened in 1931 and was founded by the Egyptian Jewish banker Ezra Mosseri.
  • It is built of locally quarried pink limestone and looks out over the Old City and Mount Zion.
  • Its guest list has included kings, presidents and film stars, among them Elizabeth Taylor and Richard Burton.
  • In July 1946 an attack on the hotel's southern wing, which housed British administration offices, became one of the best-known events of the Mandate period.

Visiting today

The hotel sits on King David Street in the centre of Jerusalem, within reach of the Old City. Ask for a room facing the walls if the view matters to you, and have a coffee on the terrace even if you stay elsewhere.
