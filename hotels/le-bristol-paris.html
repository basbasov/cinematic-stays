Le Bristol Paris: the palace hotel from Midnight in Paris

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Midnight in Paris · 2010s

Le Bristol sits on the Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré and has welcomed guests since 1925. Woody Allen's Midnight in Paris put it on screen as the hotel where a couple from California stays.

On screen: Midnight in Paris

In Midnight in Paris (2011), Le Bristol was used for the hotel-room scenes of Gil and Inez on their trip to Paris. Wikipedia lists the hotel among the film's appearances, and travel writers describe it as a location for many of the movie's scenes.

Fun facts

  • Hippolyte Jammet bought the mansion in 1923 and opened the hotel in April 1925.
  • The hotel changed hands only once, in 1978, when the Jammet family sold it to the Oetker family.
  • It is part of Oetker Collection, formerly Oetker Hotels.
  • Its rooftop swimming pool, designed by the architect Pinnau, resembles the front of a large sailboat.

Visiting today

The hotel has a garden and a pool with views over the Paris rooftops, and the luxury boutiques of Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré are on the doorstep. Check dates early, as it is a small, popular palace hotel.
