Le Meurice: the Paris palace hotel from Midnight in Paris

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Midnight in Paris, Is Paris Burning? · 2010s

Facing the Tuileries Garden on the Rue de Rivoli, Le Meurice is one of Paris's grand palace hotels and has been welcoming guests since 1815. Woody Allen's Midnight in Paris brought it back into the spotlight.

On screen: Midnight in Paris

Woody Allen shot scenes for Midnight in Paris at the hotel in 2010, and the film shows a wine tasting on its terrace and a night-time stroll through its corridors with Owen Wilson. Much earlier, the war film Is Paris Burning? (1966) was shot partly here, which suits a hotel with a real wartime history.

Fun facts

  • Charles-Augustin Meurice opened the hotel in 1815 on Rue Saint-Honoré and it moved to its current address on Rue de Rivoli in 1835.
  • Architect Henri Paul Nénot rebuilt it in the early twentieth century, keeping the original façade and giving the interiors a Louis XVI style.
  • A renovation led by designer Philippe Starck began in 2007.
  • Today it is a five-star hotel with 160 rooms, run by the Dorchester Collection.

Visiting today

The Louvre and the Tuileries are a few steps away, so the hotel is a good base for a first trip to Paris. Check the terrace and bar for afternoon or evening drinks even if you are not staying.
