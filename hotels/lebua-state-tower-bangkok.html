lebua at State Tower: the Bangkok hotel from The Hangover Part II

🌆 Bangkok, Thailand · 🎬 The Hangover Part II · 2010s

Crowned by a golden dome, the State Tower dominates Bangkok's Chao Phraya riverside. The Hangover Part II made its rooftop bar famous worldwide.

On screen: The Hangover Part II

The Sky Bar on the 63rd floor served as a filming location for The Hangover Part II. The film's Wolf Pack meets Paul Giamatti's character at one of the tower's restaurants and later returns to the Sky Bar, high above Bangkok. A cocktail called the Hangovertini was created for the cast while they filmed here.

Fun facts

  • The State Tower stands about 247 metres tall, with 68 floors, in Bangkok's Bang Rak district.
  • Its upper floors hold the Dome by lebua, a cluster of rooftop restaurants and bars including Sirocco and the Sky Bar.
  • The bar is also known as The Hangover Bar, The Dome or simply Sky Bar.
  • The hotel now offers a Hangover suite.

Visiting today

Sunset is the busiest time at the Sky Bar, and a dress code applies. Staying in the tower is the easiest way to avoid the queue.
