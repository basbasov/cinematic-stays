Lotte New York Palace: the real hotel from Gossip Girl

🗽 New York, United States · 🎬 Gossip Girl · 2000s

Behind a Gilded Age courtyard on Madison Avenue sits one of Manhattan's most photographed hotels. TV fans know it as the home of Serena van der Woodsen in Gossip Girl.

On screen: Gossip Girl

Wikipedia notes that the hotel was popularized in the 2000s by Gossip Girl, where it was depicted as the residence of Blake Lively's character Serena van der Woodsen. The hotel's own production page lists Gossip Girl among the shows and films it has hosted, along with Will & Grace, 30 Rock, Law & Order and 27 Dresses, and names the Madison Avenue courtyard as a favourite filming spot.

Fun facts

  • The hotel includes part of the Villard Houses, built in the 1880s by McKim, Mead & White around a central courtyard.
  • A 51-story tower designed by Emery Roth & Sons was completed in 1980 and rises behind the historic houses.
  • The hotel was developed between 1977 and 1980 by Harry Helmsley.
  • In the 2010s it sold Gossip Girl themed packages with memorabilia and a list of filming locations.

Visiting today

The hotel stands at 455 Madison Avenue at 50th Street, across from St. Patrick's Cathedral. The courtyard is the part most fans want to see, so ask the hotel what is open to non-guests before you go.
