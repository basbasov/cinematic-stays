Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok: the riverside hotel from The White Lotus

🌆 Bangkok, Thailand · 🎬 The White Lotus (Season 3) · 2020s

The Mandarin Oriental sits on the Chao Phraya River and is often called the first hotel in Thailand. In 2025 it welcomed the cast and crew of The White Lotus for its third season.

On screen: The White Lotus (Season 3)

Wikipedia reports that Frank's monologue to Rick in season 3 was filmed at the Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok. The hotel says it was the only Bangkok hotel chosen for the season, and that its lobby, Bamboo Bar and Verandah restaurant appear on screen. It also says its Authors' Lounge helped inspire creator Mike White's script.

Fun facts

  • The hotel counts 1876, the year of its River Wing opening, as its official founding date.
  • The original Oriental Hotel burned down in 1865, and a new building opened in 1887.
  • It was renamed Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok in September 2008.
  • Somerset Maugham is among the writers linked to the hotel.

Visiting today

The hotel is at 48 Oriental Avenue on the bank of the Chao Phraya River. Check availability early, as it is a popular address in Bangkok.
