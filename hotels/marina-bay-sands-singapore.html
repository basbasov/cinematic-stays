Marina Bay Sands: the real hotel from Crazy Rich Asians

🦁 Singapore, Singapore · 🎬 Crazy Rich Asians · 2010s

Three towers, one ship-shaped rooftop and a view over Singapore that looks almost too good for a film set. It isn't a set: Marina Bay Sands is a real hotel you can book, and its SkyPark is one of the most recognisable hotel rooftops on screen.

On screen: Crazy Rich Asians

In Crazy Rich Asians (2018), the hotel's 57th-floor SkyPark and its enormous infinity pool provide the glamorous backdrop for the film's lavish Singapore scenes. The pool stretches along the edge of the tower with the city skyline behind it, which is exactly why filmmakers love it.

Fun facts

  • Marina Bay Sands opened in 2010 and was designed by architect Moshe Safdie.
  • The SkyPark sits on top of all three towers, like a ship resting on three pillars.
  • The rooftop infinity pool is around 150 metres long, among the largest of its kind at that height.
  • The pool is reserved for hotel guests, so a booking is your ticket to the view from the film.

Visiting today

Staying here is the simplest way to swim in the famous pool. Book early for weekends, and ask for a high floor facing the bay or the Gardens by the Bay for the best views.
