The Biltmore Hotel Coral Gables: the Spanish Revival landmark on screen

🌴 Miami (Coral Gables), United States · 🎬 Bad Boys, Miami Vice · 1990s

With a tower modelled on Seville's Giralda, the Biltmore has dominated Coral Gables since 1926. Film and TV crews have long favoured it.

On screen: Bad Boys

The hotel appears in the Will Smith and Martin Lawrence action hit Bad Boys (1995) and was a recurring location for the TV series Miami Vice. Its Mediterranean architecture suits Miami's glamorous screen image.

Fun facts

  • The Biltmore opened in January 1926, designed by Schultze and Weaver, after about ten months of construction.
  • At 315 feet, it was the tallest building in Florida at the time.
  • In the 1926 hurricane, the hotel sheltered survivors.
  • During World War II it served as a veterans' hospital, and later as a University of Miami medical school until 1968.
  • It was designated a National Historic Landmark in 1986 and restored as a hotel in 1987.
  • The pool was once reported to be the largest hotel pool in the United States.

Visiting today

Coral Gables is a calm, tree-lined suburb a short drive from Miami's beaches. The golf course and pool are the highlights.
