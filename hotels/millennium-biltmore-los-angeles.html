The Biltmore Los Angeles: the Ghostbusters hotel you can book

🌴 Los Angeles, USA · 🎬 Ghostbusters · 1980s

Opened in 1923, the Biltmore is one of Los Angeles's grandest hotels, and one of Hollywood's busiest film locations. Ghostbusters fans know it as the Sedgwick Hotel.

On screen: Ghostbusters

In Ghostbusters (1984), the hotel stands in for the fictional Sedgwick Hotel, where the team takes its first ghost-catching job and Egon delivers the famous warning not to cross the streams. The ornate ballroom and lobby spaces make it an easy fit for period and fantasy settings alike.

Fun facts

  • When it opened in 1923, the Biltmore was the largest hotel west of Chicago.
  • It hosted the Academy Awards ceremony eight times between the early 1930s and 1942.
  • A full-size boxing ring was set up under the chandeliers of the Crystal Ballroom for Rocky III.
  • The hotel also appears in Chinatown, The Sting, Beverly Hills Cop and Bugsy.
  • The hotel hosted events around the 1960 Democratic National Convention in Los Angeles.

Visiting today

The Biltmore sits in downtown Los Angeles, close to the Metro and the city's museums. Walk through the lobby even if you only stop for a drink, because the painted ceilings are the highlight.
