Mount Lavinia Hotel: the Sri Lankan hotel from The Bridge on the River Kwai

🌴 Colombo, Sri Lanka · 🎬 The Bridge on the River Kwai · 1950s

Long before it became a beachfront hotel, this building near Colombo was a governor's residence. In 1957 it also took a role in David Lean's Oscar-winning war film.

On screen: The Bridge on the River Kwai

A number of scenes from The Bridge on the River Kwai (1957) were filmed at the Mount Lavinia Hotel. Film-location guides identify the building as the military hospital seen in the movie. The famous bridge itself was filmed elsewhere in Sri Lanka, not at the hotel.

Fun facts

  • The building was commissioned in 1806 as a residence for Sir Thomas Maitland, the second governor of British Ceylon.
  • It has operated continuously as a hotel since 1947.
  • Today the hotel has 275 rooms and sits at 100 Hotel Road, right on the coast.
  • The film locations guide notes that the building was known as the Governor's Mansion in colonial times.

Visiting today

Mount Lavinia is a coastal suburb south of central Colombo, so allow time for traffic if you are coming from the city or the airport. Dinner by the sea is a popular way to see the building even if you do not stay.
