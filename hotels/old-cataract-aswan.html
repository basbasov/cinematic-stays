Old Cataract Aswan: the Nile hotel behind Death on the Nile

🏜️ Aswan, Egypt · 🎬 Death on the Nile · 1970s

Perched above the Nile on a rocky bluff, the Old Cataract has been one of Egypt's most storied hotels since 1900. It is closely tied to Agatha Christie's Death on the Nile.

On screen: Death on the Nile

Tradition holds that Agatha Christie wrote part of Death on the Nile on the hotel's terrace, and the 1978 film adaptation with Peter Ustinov as Poirot is reported to have been shot here. Sources differ, and Wikipedia flags the filming claim as needing a citation.

Fun facts

  • The hotel was built in 1899 by Thomas Cook & Son and opened in January 1900.
  • It was built to serve travellers arriving on Nile cruises and the Cairo-Aswan railway.
  • A tower wing was added in 1961, and the original building became known as the Old Cataract.
  • The hotel is being rebranded as Mandarin Oriental Old Cataract, Aswan, with a full return to service planned for 2027.

Visiting today

Because of the rebranding, check current availability before planning a trip. Winter is the most comfortable season in Aswan.
