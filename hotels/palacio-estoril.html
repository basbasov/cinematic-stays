Palácio Estoril: the Bond hotel on the Portuguese Riviera

🌊 Estoril (Cascais), Portugal · 🎬 On Her Majesty's Secret Service · 1960s

Opposite the Estoril Casino, the Palácio Estoril has been a grand seaside hotel since 1930. James Bond checked in here in 1969.

On screen: On Her Majesty's Secret Service

In On Her Majesty's Secret Service (1969), the hotel serves as Bond's base. The exterior, lobby, pool and rooms were all filmed here. Ian Fleming, who arrived in Estoril in 1941 as a naval intelligence officer, is said to have drawn on the neighbouring casino for his first Bond novel, Casino Royale.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened in 1930, founded by Fausto Figueiredo and designed by Henry Martinet.
  • Portugal's neutrality in World War II drew exiled royals, some of whom stayed at the hotel.
  • The Fleming and Casino Royale story is told as tradition rather than documented fact.
  • The hotel's bar features a 007 Martini in tribute to Fleming and Bond.

Visiting today

Estoril is a short train ride from Lisbon along the coast. The casino and gardens across the road are part of the Bond trail.
