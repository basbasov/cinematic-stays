Park Hyatt Tokyo: the real hotel from Lost in Translation

🗼 Tokyo, Japan · 🎬 Lost in Translation · 2000s

High above Shinjuku, the Park Hyatt Tokyo is where Sofia Coppola's Lost in Translation (2003) found its jet-lagged, quietly romantic mood. The hotel is real, and you can book the same view.

On screen: Lost in Translation

The film's bar scenes take place in the New York Bar on the 52nd floor, where Bob and Charlotte first meet. Coppola shot on location here, though the hotel was reportedly hesitant at first when she approached it about filming on site.

Fun facts

  • The New York Bar was designed to evoke a New York jazz club.
  • The bar sits on the 52nd floor of the Shinjuku Park Tower, with floor-to-ceiling windows over the city.
  • The bar does not take reservations, so arriving early is the best way to get a window seat.
  • One guide reports that the evening cover charge is waived for hotel guests.

Visiting today

Even if you stay elsewhere, a drink at the New York Bar is the classic fan pilgrimage. Staying in the hotel gives you the high-floor Tokyo views without the queue.
