The Peabody Memphis: the duck hotel from The Firm

🎸 Memphis, USA · 🎬 The Firm · 1990s

Every day, a troop of ducks marches through the lobby of The Peabody Memphis to a fountain. It is one of America's quirkiest hotel traditions, and the hotel also has a role in a Tom Cruise thriller.

On screen: The Firm

Sydney Pollack shot part of his 1993 legal thriller The Firm at The Peabody, including a pivotal scene with Tom Cruise and Jeanne Tripplehorn on the roof. The grand lobby and Southern setting give the film its sense of old-money Memphis.

Fun facts

  • The ducks began in 1933, when general manager Frank Schutt returned from a hunting trip and put live decoy ducks in the lobby fountain as a prank.
  • The duck march currently takes place at 11 a.m. and 5 p.m. every day of the year.
  • The hotel was founded in 1869 and moved to its present building in 1925.
  • It closed in the mid-1970s during Memphis's economic decline and reopened in 1981.

Visiting today

Arrive before 11 a.m. or 5 p.m. to watch the march from the lobby. The hotel sits downtown, a short walk from Beale Street.
