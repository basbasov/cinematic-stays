Hôtel Plaza Athénée: the Paris hotel from Sex and the City

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Sex and the City, Something's Gotta Give, Emily in Paris · 2000s

With its red awnings and geranium-filled window boxes, the Plaza Athénée is one of Paris's easiest hotels to recognise, and it is a screen favourite.

On screen: Sex and the City

The hotel appears in Sex and the City, Something's Gotta Give and Emily in Paris. In the Sex and the City Paris storyline, Carrie Bradshaw stays here, with an Eiffel Tower view from the balcony.

Fun facts

  • The current building on Avenue Montaigne opened in April 1913 and was constructed in two phases between 1912 and 1919.
  • An earlier Grand Hôtel Athénée opened on Rue Scribe in 1867.
  • After the war, it became popular with Hollywood stars and royalty.
  • In 2008, Dior opened the Dior Institut at the hotel.
  • In 2011 it was one of only five Paris hotels given the Palace distinction.

Visiting today

The hotel is on Avenue Montaigne, Paris's luxury shopping street, close to the Eiffel Tower. Rooms with a tower view are the most requested.
