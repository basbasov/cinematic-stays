The Plaza New York: the real hotel from Home Alone 2

🗽 New York, USA · 🎬 Home Alone 2, Sleepless in Seattle · 1990s

Marble lobby, Central Park views and a Fifth Avenue address: The Plaza has been a New York icon since 1907. For a generation of film fans it is also the hotel where Kevin McCallister charmed the concierge and ordered room service in Home Alone 2: Lost in New York.

On screen: Home Alone 2

In the 1992 sequel, Kevin ends up in New York while his family flies to Florida, and checks into The Plaza with his dad's credit card. The lobby and the hotel's grand interiors appear throughout the film, along with a brief cameo by the hotel's then-owner, Donald Trump.

Fun facts

  • The Plaza opened in 1907 on Fifth Avenue at the southeast corner of Central Park.
  • Director Chris Columbus has said the Trump cameo was a condition for filming at the hotel; Trump has disputed that.
  • The hotel appears in many other films, including Alfred Hitchcock's North by Northwest (1959).
  • It is also the home of Eloise, the fictional little girl from Kay Thompson's children's books.

Visiting today

Today's Plaza is a luxury hotel with a landmark lobby, the Palm Court and rooms facing the park. Park-view rooms book out quickly in December, when the film is on everyone's watchlist.
