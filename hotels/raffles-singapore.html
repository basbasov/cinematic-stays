Raffles Singapore: the Kingsford Hotel from Crazy Rich Asians

🦁 Singapore, Singapore · 🎬 Crazy Rich Asians · 2010s

Opened in the 1880s and named after Sir Stamford Raffles, Raffles Singapore is the city's grande dame. Crazy Rich Asians used it as the fictional Kingsford Hotel.

On screen: Crazy Rich Asians

In Crazy Rich Asians (2018), Raffles Hotel is the Kingsford, where Nick and Rachel stay when she isn't welcome at his family's home. Some guides place their stay in the hotel's presidential suite, and the filming took place before the hotel's refurbishment began.

Fun facts

  • Raffles opened in 1887, though some listings give 1886, and was made a National Monument in 1987.
  • The Singapore Sling is linked to bartender Ngiam Tong Boon, who is said to have created it at the Long Bar in the early 20th century.
  • A widely repeated story says the last tiger shot in Singapore died under the Bar and Billiard Room in 1902.
  • Guests over the years included Rudyard Kipling and Charlie Chaplin.
  • The Long Bar reopened in November 2018 after a restoration lasting about two and a half years.

Visiting today

The Long Bar, with its peanut shells on the floor, is the traditional place for a Singapore Sling. Raffles reopened after its refurbishment in 2019.
