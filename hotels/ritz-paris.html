Hôtel Ritz Paris: the real hotel from Love in the Afternoon

🗼 Paris, France · 🎬 Love in the Afternoon · 1950s

Opened in 1898 on Place Vendôme, the Ritz has been Paris's byword for luxury ever since. Billy Wilder set much of Love in the Afternoon here.

On screen: Love in the Afternoon

In Love in the Afternoon (1957), Audrey Hepburn's character begins her romance with Gary Cooper's wealthy American in his suite at the Ritz. The hotel also appears in William Wyler's 1966 comedy How to Steal a Million.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on June 1, 1898, founded by César Ritz with the chef Auguste Escoffier, in two 18th-century palaces.
  • Coco Chanel lived in a suite here for decades, and the Coco Chanel Suite is decorated in her favourite black and white.
  • Bar Hemingway is named after Ernest Hemingway, who legend says was the first uniformed American to order a drink here after the 1944 liberation of Paris.
  • The hotel has become a symbol of Parisian luxury worldwide.

Visiting today

The hotel is a short walk from the Tuileries Garden and the Louvre. Bar Hemingway is a good way to see the interiors if a room is out of budget.
