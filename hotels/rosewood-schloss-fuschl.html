Schloss Fuschl: the castle from the Sissi films

🏰 Fuschl am See, Salzburg, Austria · 🎬 Sissi · 1950s

On a peninsula in Lake Fuschl near Salzburg, Schloss Fuschl is a castle turned hotel. It is best known as the home of the 1955 Sissi films with Romy Schneider.

On screen: Sissi

The castle was a starring location in the 1955 Sissi film trilogy, with Schneider playing Empress Elisabeth. It stood in for the family residence, with the director choosing it because the original location was not idyllic enough. Schneider stayed here during filming, and the Kaiserin Elisabeth Suite honours her stay.

Fun facts

  • The castle was built in 1461 as a hunting lodge for the Prince-Archbishops of Salzburg.
  • It was reimagined as a castle hotel in 1950.
  • The hotel has been under heritage protection since 1981.
  • A complete renovation began in 2022 before it relaunched as Rosewood Schloss Fuschl.
  • The hotel includes a Sissi exhibition honouring Empress Elisabeth.

Visiting today

The lake and the Salzkammergut hills are the main draw, and Salzburg is a short drive away. Summer and Christmas market season are the busiest periods.
