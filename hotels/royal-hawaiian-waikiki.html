The Royal Hawaiian: the pink Waikiki hotel from Mad Men and Big Eyes

🌺 Honolulu (Waikiki), United States · 🎬 Big Eyes, Punch-Drunk Love, Mad Men · 2010s

With its pink walls and palm-lined gardens, The Royal Hawaiian has stood on Waikiki Beach since 1927. It is easy to see why film and TV crews keep coming back to the Pink Palace of the Pacific.

On screen: Big Eyes

In Big Eyes (2014), the hotel is where Margaret and Walter Keane, played by Amy Adams and Christoph Waltz, are married. In the sixth-season premiere of Mad Men (2013), Don and Megan Draper honeymoon here and attend a luau. The hotel also appears in Punch-Drunk Love (2002), where a photo of a kiss in one of its archways was used for the film's poster, and its exterior turned up in the original Hawaii Five-O.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on 1 February 1927 with a black-tie gala for more than 1,200 guests.
  • New York architects Warren and Wetmore designed it in a Spanish and Moorish style with pink stucco walls.
  • It closed in June 2008 for a major renovation and reopened in January 2009.
  • Joni Mitchell's song Big Yellow Taxi refers to it as the pink hotel.

Visiting today

The hotel has more than 500 rooms and sits right on the beach, so it works as a base for a Waikiki holiday. Look out for the evening luau if you want a taste of the Mad Men honeymoon scene.
