Salish Lodge & Spa: the real Great Northern Hotel from Twin Peaks

🌲 Snoqualmie, USA · 🎬 Twin Peaks · 1990s

Perched above Snoqualmie Falls, about 30 minutes east of Seattle, Salish Lodge & Spa is instantly familiar to fans of Twin Peaks. It is the building that stood in for the Great Northern Hotel on screen.

On screen: Twin Peaks

David Lynch's series used the lodge's exterior as the Great Northern, and the hotel appears above the falls in the opening credits. The hotel interiors were not shot here: they were filmed at Kiana Lodge in Poulsbo, Washington, and later on soundstages in Los Angeles. In the story, FBI agent Dale Cooper makes the hotel his base, staying in the fictional Room 315.

Fun facts

  • The property began in 1916 as an eight-room trapper's cabin.
  • It grew into the Snoqualmie Falls Lodge, and a 1988 refurbishment renamed it the Salish.
  • The Snoqualmie Indian Tribe became the owner in 2019, and the hotel has 86 rooms.
  • Many rooms look out over the 268-foot Snoqualmie Falls.

Visiting today

Twin Peaks fans can stay in a falls-view room and walk to the viewing area nearby. The lodge is an easy drive from Seattle, which makes it a good weekend trip.
