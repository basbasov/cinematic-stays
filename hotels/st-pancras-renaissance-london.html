St Pancras Renaissance London: the Gothic hotel from Batman Begins

🎡 London, United Kingdom · 🎬 Batman Begins, The Secret Garden · 2000s

Next to St Pancras station, the red-brick Gothic Revival hotel looks like a film set, and film-makers have treated it as one. It was built as the Midland Grand Hotel and reopened as a hotel in 2011.

On screen: Batman Begins

The hotel's staircase doubled for the Arkham Asylum stairwell in Christopher Nolan's Batman Begins (2005). Its grand staircase also served as Mistlethwaite Manor in The Secret Garden (1993), though the house exteriors and most interiors of that film were shot elsewhere.

Fun facts

  • In 1865 the Midland Railway held a competition for a hotel beside the station, won by George Gilbert Scott.
  • Scott's design was much larger and costlier than the brief asked for.
  • The Midland Grand opened in 1873 and operated as a hotel only until 1935, when it became railway offices.
  • It reopened as the St Pancras Renaissance Hotel in 2011 after a major restoration.
  • In 2025 it moved to Marriott's Autograph Collection and was renamed St. Pancras London.

Visiting today

The Eurostar terminal is next door, which makes it an easy base for trips to Paris. The staircase is the highlight, so book a stay or an afternoon tea to see it.
