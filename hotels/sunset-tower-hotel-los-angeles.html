Sunset Tower Hotel: the Art Deco landmark from The Player and Get Shorty

🌴 West Hollywood, United States · 🎬 The Player, Get Shorty · 1990s

Rising above Sunset Boulevard since 1931, the Sunset Tower is one of the most photogenic Art Deco buildings in Los Angeles. Film fans may recognise it from a pool scene in Robert Altman's The Player.

On screen: The Player

Visit West Hollywood notes that a pivotal scene in The Player (1992) was shot by the hotel pool, and that the building also appears in Get Shorty (1995). Wikipedia lists further screen credits, including the TV series Cannon, where the tower is the lead character's home, and exterior shots in Lucifer.

Fun facts

  • The tower was designed by Leland A. Bryant, completed in August 1931 and opened as a luxury apartment hotel.
  • It is 15 stories tall and decorated with plaster friezes of plants, animals, zeppelins and even Adam and Eve.
  • It was among the first reinforced-concrete high-rises in California.
  • The building is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Visiting today

Today the building is a boutique hotel on the Sunset Strip, close to West Hollywood's restaurants and nightlife. Look up at the facade before you go in; the friezes are easy to miss from the street.
