Taj Lake Palace: the floating palace from Octopussy

🛶 Udaipur, India · 🎬 Octopussy · 1980s

A white marble palace that seems to float on Lake Pichola, the Taj Lake Palace is one of India's most photographed hotels. James Bond fans know it from Octopussy (1983).

On screen: Octopussy

Octopussy, released in 1983, used Udaipur for the home of its title character, and the Lake Palace is widely identified as her lair. Some scenes also used Jag Mandir, another palace on Lake Pichola, mostly for exteriors.

Fun facts

  • The palace was built between about 1743 and 1746 for Maharana Jagat Singh II.
  • It was originally called Jag Niwas, in honour of the Maharana.
  • Stories say the Maharana built it for moonlit picnics away from the busy City Palace, though historians treat this as unproven.
  • The palace was later restored and converted into Udaipur's first luxury hotel, and is run by the Taj group.

Visiting today

Guests reach the island by boat, and the hotel is easiest to see from a lakeside viewpoint in town. Winter is the most comfortable season in Rajasthan.
