The Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai: the real hotel from Hotel Mumbai

🌆 Mumbai, India · 🎬 Hotel Mumbai · 2010s

Opposite the Gateway of India, the Taj Mahal Palace has been a Mumbai landmark since 1903. Anthony Maras's 2018 film Hotel Mumbai put it on screen as the centre of its story.

On screen: Hotel Mumbai

Hotel Mumbai, starring Dev Patel and Armie Hammer, follows the November 2008 attack on the hotel. According to the film's own production notes from Bleecker Street Media, many of the hotel's exteriors were shot on location and altered digitally, while the interiors were recreated at Adelaide Studios in Australia. Wikipedia also lists the hotel as a shooting location for Christopher Nolan's Tenet.

Fun facts

  • The hotel was commissioned by Jamsetji Tata and opened to guests on 16 December 1903.
  • During World War I it was converted into a hospital with 600 beds.
  • The attacks of 26 to 29 November 2008 hit the hotel, and its less-damaged sections reopened on 21 December 2008.
  • In March 2010 the hotel dropped the word Tower from its name and became The Taj Mahal Palace.

Visiting today

The hotel faces the harbour next to the Gateway of India in Colaba, so the famous exterior is easy to see even if you stay elsewhere. Book a sea-facing room early in the dry season, when Mumbai is busiest.
