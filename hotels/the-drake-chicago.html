The Drake Chicago: the real hotel from Risky Business and My Best Friend's Wedding

🌆 Chicago, United States · 🎬 Risky Business, My Best Friend's Wedding · 1990s

The Drake has stood at the top of Michigan Avenue since 1920, with its pink neon sign facing Lake Michigan. Two much-loved films sent their characters through its doors.

On screen: Risky Business

The movie-locations website records that in Risky Business (1983) Tom Cruise's Joel meets Rebecca De Mornay's Lana in the hotel's Palm Court. In My Best Friend's Wedding (1997) Julianne stays at the Drake, and George, played by Rupert Everett, turns up at her room. Wikipedia adds that scenes from several other films were filmed or at least set at the hotel.

Fun facts

  • The Drake opened in 1920 and was designed by architects Benjamin Marshall and Charles Eli Fox.
  • It was added to the National Register of Historic Places on May 8, 1980.
  • The hotel is known for its afternoon tea in the Palm Court.
  • In the film Mission: Impossible, the character Jim Phelps is mentioned as having stayed at the Drake shortly before the story begins.

Visiting today

The hotel is at 140 East Walton Place, at the top of the Magnificent Mile, within walking distance of Michigan Avenue shopping and Lake Michigan. Afternoon tea is a pleasant way to see the Palm Court even if you stay elsewhere.
