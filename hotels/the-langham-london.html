The Langham, London: the hotel from GoldenEye

🇬🇧 London, United Kingdom · 🎬 GoldenEye, Burnt · 1990s

The Langham opened in 1865 beside Regent Street and is one of the oldest grand hotels in London. Its entrance became a Russian hotel in the James Bond film GoldenEye.

On screen: GoldenEye

In GoldenEye (1995), the Langham's exterior was used as a stand-in for the Grand Hotel Europe in St Petersburg. Only the outside was filmed at the hotel, and the interior scenes were shot on a studio set. The hotel has also been used for the chef drama Burnt, starring Bradley Cooper.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on 10 June 1865 and was opened by the Prince of Wales.
  • It was the largest and most modern hotel in London at the time, with the first hydraulic lifts in England.
  • The BBC took over the building after the Second World War and bought it outright in 1965, and it reopened as a hotel in 1991.
  • The Palm Court has served afternoon tea since 1865, and Arthur Conan Doyle set parts of his Sherlock Holmes stories at the hotel.

Visiting today

The Langham stands at the top of Regent Street, close to Oxford Circus. Afternoon tea in the Palm Court is a good way to see the hotel even if you are not staying.
