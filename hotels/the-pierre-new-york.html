The Pierre New York: the real hotel from Scent of a Woman

🗽 New York, United States · 🎬 Scent of a Woman, For Love or Money · 1990s

The Pierre has faced Central Park from Fifth Avenue since 1930. Its Cotillion Ballroom is the setting for one of cinema's best-loved dances, Al Pacino's tango in Scent of a Woman.

On screen: Scent of a Woman

Wikipedia's film page lists the Pierre's Cotillion Ballroom as the location where Frank Slade and a young woman dance the tango in Scent of a Woman (1992). A fan site that visited the room reports that it was dressed as a restaurant for the shoot and is still recognisable today. The hotel was also the main setting of the 1993 comedy For Love or Money, where it played the fictional Bradbury Hotel.

Fun facts

  • The Pierre opened in October 1930 and was designed by the New York firm Schultze & Weaver.
  • Its blond-brick tower rises from a limestone base in the Louis XVI style.
  • The top floors were modeled on Mansart's Royal Chapel at Versailles.
  • The Cotillion Ballroom originally served as a supper club.

Visiting today

The hotel sits on the corner of Fifth Avenue and 61st Street, right across from Central Park. Rooms on the park side have the best views.
