The Savoy London: the real hotel from Notting Hill

🎡 London, United Kingdom · 🎬 Notting Hill · 1990s

The Savoy has stood on the Strand since 1889 and has been a London landmark ever since. Film fans know it best as the setting for the finale of Notting Hill.

On screen: Notting Hill

In Notting Hill (1999), Julia Roberts's film star Anna Scott gives a press conference in the hotel's Lancaster Room, and Hugh Grant's William interrupts it to win her back. Wikipedia and a 2026 travel feature in The Week place this scene at the Savoy, and the hotel itself mentions the press conference scene on its page for the room, now called the Lancaster Ballroom.

Fun facts

  • The Savoy opened on 6 August 1889, built by impresario Richard D'Oyly Carte with profits from Gilbert and Sullivan operas.
  • Thomas Edward Collcutt was the architect.
  • It was the first hotel in Britain lit by electric light and the first to have electric lifts.
  • The building is Grade II listed and today has 267 guest rooms.
