Waldorf Astoria New York: the real hotel from Scent of a Woman

🗽 New York, United States · 🎬 Week-End at the Waldorf, Scent of a Woman · 1990s

Few hotels have been on screen as often as the Waldorf Astoria, the Art Deco tower on Park Avenue that has been a Manhattan landmark since 1931. After a long closure for restoration, you can book a room in the building again.

On screen: Week-End at the Waldorf

The 1945 comedy-drama Week-End at the Waldorf is set among the guests and staff of the hotel and was partly filmed on location there. Decades later, Scent of a Woman (1992) sent Al Pacino's blind retired colonel and his young companion to the Waldorf for a luxurious New York weekend. Wikipedia lists many more films shot at the hotel, among them Coming to America, The Royal Tenenbaums and Serendipity.

Fun facts

  • The 47-story tower was designed by Schultze and Weaver and finished in 1931.
  • It was the world's tallest hotel until 1957.
  • The original Waldorf-Astoria stood on Fifth Avenue and was torn down in 1929 to make room for the Empire State Building.
  • Television series such as Sex and the City and The Sopranos have also filmed at the Waldorf.

Visiting today

The hotel closed in 2017 and has reopened after a major restoration, with 375 hotel rooms in the lower floors and private residences above. Check the booking page for current rates and room types.
