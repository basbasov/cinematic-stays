The Westin Grand Berlin: the Bourne Supremacy staircase

🐻 Berlin, Germany · 🎬 The Bourne Supremacy · 2000s

On Friedrichstrasse in central Berlin, the Westin Grand has a lobby staircase worthy of a thriller, and the Bourne franchise noticed.

On screen: The Bourne Supremacy

In The Bourne Supremacy (2004), the hotel is where CIA official Pamela Landy is staying, and Jason Bourne makes his way through the foyer and up the grand staircase to reach her. Visit Berlin says Matt Damon and Franka Potente spent about a week here filming key scenes.

Fun facts

  • The hotel opened on August 1, 1987, as the Grand Hotel Berlin.
  • It was the showcase hotel of East Germany's state-run hotel chain.
  • The atrium is a large octagonal space seven storeys high, with a coloured glass ceiling.
  • The building sits on Friedrichstrasse, close to Checkpoint Charlie.
  • Marriott says the grand staircase and lobby were prominently featured in the film.

Visiting today

The hotel is central, close to Unter den Linden and the Brandenburg Gate, and easy to reach by U-Bahn. Walk through the lobby even if you are not staying, to see the staircase from the film.
