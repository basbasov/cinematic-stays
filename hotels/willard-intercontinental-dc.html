Willard InterContinental Washington: the Minority Report hotel

🏛️ Washington, USA · 🎬 Minority Report · 2000s

A block from the White House, the Willard has been part of Washington life for generations. Steven Spielberg chose it for the finale of Minority Report.

On screen: Minority Report

Spielberg filmed the climax of Minority Report at the Willard in summer 2001. Tom Cruise and Max von Sydow play out their confrontation in the Willard Room, with scenes also shot along Peacock Alley and in the kitchen.

Fun facts

  • The hotel is a Beaux-Arts landmark at 1401 Pennsylvania Avenue NW and sits on the D.C. Inventory of Historic Sites.
  • The Willard has been central to Washington's social and political scene since it opened in 1818.
  • A fire damaged the building in 1922, and it was restored in the late 1990s.
  • Peacock Alley is named after the promenade at New York's Waldorf-Astoria, and now serves traditional high tea.
  • The word lobbying is often said to have been coined in the hotel's lobby, though accounts vary.

Visiting today

Many of the hotel's rooms are close to the National Mall and the White House. Afternoon tea in Peacock Alley is the easiest way to see the film's setting.
