Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026. The short version: we do not ask for accounts or personal details from visitors.

What we collect

Cinematic Stays does not require registration and does not collect names, emails, or payment details from visitors. Our hosting provider (Netlify) may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and reliability.

Cookies and analytics

The site itself does not set tracking cookies. If we add analytics later, we will update this page and use privacy-respecting tools where possible.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and hotel photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. Their servers may receive your IP address when your browser fetches those files. Hotel data is stored with Supabase when the admin backend is enabled.

Links to other sites

"Check availability" buttons lead to Booking.com, a separate website with its own privacy policy and cookies. We are not responsible for what happens on third-party sites.

Your rights and contact

If you have questions about your data or want something removed, email dw.basov@gmail.com. We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
